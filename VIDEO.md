## Recording script (≈ 4:45)

**Setup:** iPhone (Expo Go, app open) next to a laptop showing `http://localhost:8081/family`.
Record the phone with iOS Screen Recording (Control Center → ⏺) and the laptop with QuickTime
(File → New Screen Recording), or film both with a second phone. Before you start: dashboard →
Live feed → **Reset demo**; set a task for ~2 minutes after you plan to reach scene 5.
