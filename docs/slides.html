What we built

Two apps, one family. Working today.

Parent app · "Amma" (iPhone)

  • Talk: "I took my BP tablet", "Call my son", "Chest pain" → done in ~2 s
  • Today's plan: loud spoken alarm, confirm by voice, walk step counter
  • Health checks on the phone's sensors: breathing, chair stand, memory, heart rate
  • Scam check: paste or screenshot a message → verdict + 1930
  • Food & cab by voice, with the son's approval; OTP read aloud

Family dashboard · "Ravi" (web)

  • Live feed of what Amma did, with red alerts for emergencies and scams
  • Care plan: set tasks that ring on her phone; see voice confirmations
  • Approve food carts; track rides and completions
  • Body health tracking + Care tips: results → advice → "Assign to Amma"
  • Bujji score: measured accuracy vs a standard speech model
Honest status

Food orders and cab rides are demo flows (no real order/ride). Heart-rate and accuracy numbers still need real-person testing. Wellness, not diagnosis.

→
4

AI understanding

→
5

Action + family

Reminder, call, alert or order; live on the dashboard

Phone = the device

Motion sensor, microphone, pedometer and GPS power the checks. No wearable to buy.

Private by design

API keys stay on the server. Health audio is analysed on the phone and never uploaded.
